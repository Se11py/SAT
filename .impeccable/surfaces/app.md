# Surface brief: SAT Math practice app

Scope: the whole app (map home, practice session, review). Mode: Operate. Code-led build.

Audience and task: solo students drilling Hard SAT Math questions in short sessions. They pick a skill, answer, check, read the rationale, move on. Content: 610 images-based questions, 4 domains, 19 skills, MCQ and student-produced response.

Unresolved: accounts and sync, timed mode, score prediction, light theme, deployment target.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Midnight Transit Diagram. The four SAT domains are four coloured rail lines, the 19 skills are stations, and practising is riding to the next stop. The surface refuses the dashboard of progress rings and stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight-blue enamel shell (#0d1b3d family) with porcelain-white question panels, because the question images are black on white. Four fixed line inks: scarlet Algebra, cobalt Advanced Math, amber Problem-Solving and Data Analysis, green Geometry and Trigonometry. Lines bend only at 45 and 90 degrees. Station ticks are circles: hollow, ringed or filled. One humanist sans (Atkinson Hyperlegible) for everything. Standard web controls.

STORY: A student sees at once which lines they have travelled and which stations remain, picks a station or takes the suggested next stop, and works through a set. They believe the map because every station count is real. They act by pressing Start.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with product name and nav (Map, Review). Left, about 60% wide: the network diagram, four lines converging on a central Mixed interchange, stations labelled with solved/total. Right: a "Next stop" panel with the suggested skill, its line colour, a Start set button, and totals (solved, accuracy, missed). Primary action sits top of the right panel.

FORM: Transit diagram challenger, competitive verdict, user-chosen. seed: seed:6e69812ee25c2488
